# Dr. Cherise Dunn Personal Website

This repository contains the personal website for **Dr. Cherise Dunn** — founder-operator, strategic advisor, and healthcare innovation leader working across responsible AI adoption, digital manufacturing, health equity, and implementation-ready health innovation capability.

Live site:  
https://cherisedunn.github.io/personal-page/

---

## About the Website

This website is designed as a senior personal brand and credibility asset for Dr. Cherise Dunn.

It positions Dr. Dunn as a founder-operator and strategic advisor helping institutions move healthcare innovation from ambition, pilot activity, or partnership intent into practical implementation models.

The site is intended for serious institutional audiences, including:

- Health systems
- Public institutions
- Universities and research partners
- Funders and innovation programmes
- Strategic partners
- Boards and advisory committees
- Media, conferences, and convening platforms

---

## Strategic Positioning

The website is built around the central thesis:

> Healthcare innovation rarely fails because the idea alone is weak. It often fails because the operating model is unclear.

Dr. Dunn’s work connects responsible AI adoption, resilient health manufacturing, health equity, public health, and cross-sector implementation.

The site presents her as best suited for institutions working to turn health technologies, partnerships, and equity commitments into governed, fundable, and implementable models.

## Design Direction

The website is designed to feel:

- Premium
- Calm
- Editorial
- Trustworthy
- Senior
- Commercially credible
- Clear and easy to scan
- Mobile-friendly
- Not overdesigned
- Not SaaS-like
- Not flashy or cluttered

The design prioritises credibility, clarity, and conversion over visual noise.

---

## Content Principles

The website copy follows these principles:

- Clear over clever
- Specific over generic
- Credible over inflated
- Implementation-led over buzzword-driven
- Institutional over personal-brand hype
- Calm confidence over self-promotion

The site avoids vague language such as:

- “Driving transformation”
- “Empowering change”
- “Thought leader”
- “Game-changing”
- “Disruptive”
- “Innovation ecosystem”
- “AI fluency”

Instead, the copy focuses on practical institutional language such as:

- Responsible AI adoption
- Implementation readiness
- Resilient health manufacturing
- Health equity and access
- Partnership governance
- Point-of-care production
- Cross-sector implementation
- Institutional advisory

---

## Quality Checklist Before Publishing

Before deploying updates, check:

- No unverified claims have been added
- No inflated or generic language has been introduced
- All links work correctly
- All images have meaningful alt text
- Mobile layout is clean and readable
- Contact form remains functional
- Public phone numbers are not exposed unless intentionally required
- Section spacing remains consistent
- CTAs remain clear and easy to find
- The site still feels calm, senior, and credible
